Cube 27 — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

In short: Cube 27 plays fully offline and collects nothing in that state. Only when leaderboards are on do your scores and the display name you choose get sent to our server. No account, email or phone number is required. You may optionally link your progress to your Google Play Games (Android) or Game Center (iOS) account so it survives a reinstall; that is entirely your choice. To understand how the app is used, Google Analytics for Firebase is used when your privacy choices allow it (see "Third-party services"). While leaderboards are on, usage and performance data (for example session length, which screen the game was closed on, and the device's frame rate) is sent to our own server to improve the game; this server data is not used for advertising and never sold. The app uses Google AdMob to show ads; AdMob may collect data such as the advertising ID, IP address and in-app interactions and share it with Google (see the "Advertising" section). Your data is never sold. You can turn leaderboards off and delete your server-side data from inside the app.

Data we collect

The following is sent from your device to our server only while leaderboards are on (on by default, switchable in Settings):

The app has no in-app purchases, and does not collect precise location (GPS), contacts, photos, email, phone number or crash logs (only the country code above is stored). Because the app shows ads, Google AdMob may collect the data listed in the "Advertising" section and share it with Google; when you allow it, Google Analytics for Firebase and, on Android, TikTok process the data listed in "Third-party services". Other than that, your data is never shared with third parties or sold.

Turning leaderboards off

With Settings → Leaderboards off, no score is sent and the app does not talk to our server at all. The whole game — endless, adventure and Daily Cube — works exactly the same.

Deleting your data

Settings → Delete my data removes your server record completely and irreversibly: your display name, all your scores on all three boards, your usage and performance data, your gold and costume record, your player record and, if you linked one, your Play Games or Game Center account link. Leaderboards then turn off automatically, so your next game does not re-register you.

Your local best score, statistics and adventure progress stay on your device; remove them by uninstalling the app or clearing its data in your device settings.

If you no longer have access to the app, you can also request deletion at saidari2021@gmail.com. Step-by-step instructions and exactly what is deleted or kept: Data Deletion page.

Usage data sent to Google Analytics for Firebase is not linked to your server record, name or account, so "Delete my data" does not cover it. Google keeps it for at most 14 months. In regions where the app shows a consent screen you can withdraw analytics consent under Settings → Ad preferences; uninstalling the app also removes the analytics identifier on the device. You can write to us to request deletion of such data.

Stored on your device

This is kept only in the app's own storage and never leaves the device while leaderboards are off:

  • Your best score and lifetime statistics (games, moves, time, largest tile)
  • Your adventure progress and stars
  • Sound and vibration preferences, interface language, theme
  • Your unfinished game (so you can resume)
  • Whether you played today's Daily Cube, and your streak

Where data is held

The server runs on Cloudflare Workers and Cloudflare D1. Connections are encrypted with HTTPS. Cloudflare acts as our service provider for this processing and does not use your data for its own purposes.

Retention

Scores and your player record are kept so the boards work, until you delete them. Individual usage events are deleted automatically after 90 days; only totals such as overall play time and session count remain. Abuse counters (the salted IP hash) are useful for less than a month and are cleared regularly.

Children's privacy

The app is designed for players aged 13 and over, and ads are limited to content suitable for that age group. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13; the only "personal" field collected is the display name the player types, and it is optional. If you notice that your child has entered a name, you can delete it from Settings or write to us.

Third-party services

The app uses Google AdMob to show ads (see the "Advertising" section for details).

To understand app usage and advertising performance, the app uses Google Analytics for Firebase. When your privacy choices allow it, Google/Firebase may process: game and screen interactions (for example starting and finishing a game, tiles reached, opening the shop and leaderboards), app version and language, broad install source/campaign categories, timing and error categories, ad impression and revenue information, the app-instance identifier and device information, and approximate location derived from the IP address. The Android advertising ID may be used subject to the applicable permissions; on iOS, Analytics does not collect the advertising identifier (IDFA). We do not send your player name, account identifier or raw campaign/click identifiers to Firebase. Analytics collection is turned on only when the consent result permits it.

On Android, the app uses the TikTok Business SDK so that TikTok ads can measure how many people install the app. It starts only when your privacy choices allow advertising storage and advertising data; otherwise it does not run at all. Once started, TikTok may process: app installs and opens, a few game moments (finishing your first game, reaching the 256 and 512 tiles), interstitial or rewarded ad views, the amount of in-game gold spent, the Android advertising ID, device and app information and your IP address. We do not send your player name, account identifier or score records to TikTok. This data is governed by the TikTok Privacy Policy.

Account linking uses Google Play Games Services on Android and Apple's Game Center on iOS. Your record is associated with these accounts only if you choose to link. Linking does not send your game data to Google or Apple; the connection is one-way and serves only to verify your identity. If you are already signed in to Game Center on your device, Apple may verify your session automatically when the app opens and show a "welcome back" notice; that happens between your device and Apple. These exchanges are governed by the Google Privacy Policy and the Apple Privacy Policy.

Advertising

Cube 27 uses Google AdMob to show ads: a banner at the bottom of the screen, an occasional interstitial between games, and rewarded ads that you watch only if you choose to (for example to double your reward). To serve these ads, AdMob's code in the app (the Google Mobile Ads SDK) may automatically collect the following from your device and share it with Google: your IP address (which may be used to estimate the device's approximate location), in-app interactions (app launches, taps, ad views), diagnostic information (how the app and the SDK perform, for example launch time) and advertising/device identifiers (on Android the advertising ID and app set ID; on iOS the advertising ID only if you allow it).
