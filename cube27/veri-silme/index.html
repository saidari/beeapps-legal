Cube 27 — Veri Silme

Bee Apps · com.beeapps.cube27 · Son güncelleme: 23 Eylül 2026

Bu sayfa, Bee Apps tarafından yayınlanan Cube 27 uygulamasında toplanan verilerin nasıl sildirileceğini anlatır. Silme işlemi uygulamanın içinden, tek bir onayla yapılır ve geri alınamaz.

Uygulamadan silme (önerilen)

  1. Cube 27'yi aç.
  2. Ana ekranda Ayarlar'a dokun.
  3. Kartın altındaki Verilerimi sil yazısına dokun.
  4. Açılan onay ekranında Sil'e dokun.

Silme anında yapılır; ayrıca bir onay beklemene gerek yoktur. İşlemden sonra liderlik tabloları kendiliğinden kapanır, yani bir sonraki oyunun seni yeniden kaydetmez. Dilersen Ayarlar → Liderlik anahtarıyla istediğin zaman geri açabilirsin.

E-posta ile silme talebi

Uygulamaya erişimin yoksa (örneğin telefonu kaybettiysen) saidari2021@gmail.com adresine yazabilirsin. Kaydını bulabilmemiz için tabloda görünen adını belirtmen yeterlidir. Talepler 30 gün içinde sonuçlandırılır.

Cube 27 — Data Deletion

Bee Apps · com.beeapps.cube27 · Last updated: 23 September 2026

This page explains how to have the data collected by Cube 27, published by Bee Apps, deleted. Deletion is done from inside the app with a single confirmation and is irreversible.

Delete from the app (recommended)

  1. Open Cube 27.
  2. On the main screen, tap Settings.
  3. At the bottom of the card, tap Delete my data.
  4. On the confirmation screen, tap Delete.

Deletion happens immediately; no further approval is needed. Afterwards leaderboards turn off automatically, so your next game does not re-register you. You can turn them back on any time with Settings → Leaderboards.

Requesting deletion by email

If you no longer have access to the app (for example you lost the phone), write to saidari2021@gmail.com. Include the display name you used on the boards so we can find your record. Requests are completed within 30 days.

What is deleted and what is kept

DataWhat happens
Your display name Deleted — immediately and permanently. The name becomes available again.
All your scores and move records on the Daily, Endless and Adventure boards Deleted — immediately and permanently. You disappear from the boards.
Your player record and anonymous device key Deleted — immediately and permanently.
Your usage and performance data (sessions, events, frame-time summary) and your gold and costume record Deleted — immediately and permanently.
Abuse counter (a salted, irreversible hash of your IP address) Unlinked from you; the counter itself remains and is deleted automatically after at most 30 days. It cannot be tied to your identity and exists to stop automated account creation from one place. Removing it on deletion would make the deletion feature itself the way around that protection.
Your local best score, statistics, adventure progress and preferences Stay with you — these are never sent to the server. Remove them by uninstalling the app or clearing its data in your device settings.

Backups

Deleted records may remain in our database provider's operational backups for up to 30 days. Those backups are kept for disaster recovery only, are not queried, and roll out of the cycle automatically.
